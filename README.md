## 🎯 Project Title

**Web-Based Dairy Milk Billing and Management System**

---

## 📖 Overview

This project is a web-based platform designed to automate dairy business operations including milk purchase, sales, and billing management. It eliminates manual record-keeping and reduces calculation errors by handling pricing based on fat content, CLR, SNF, and quantity.

---

## 🚀 Features

* Automated milk price calculation
* Supplier and customer management
* Milk purchase and sales tracking
* Advance payment tracking
* Morning and evening record management
* PDF bill generation
* Financial summaries and reporting

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* **Frontend:** React.js, Vite, Tailwind CSS
* **Backend:** Node.js, Express.js
* **Database:** PostgreSQL (Prisma ORM)
* **Other:** REST APIs, Authentication, PDF Generation
